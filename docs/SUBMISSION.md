## Brief description draft

Vajra helps residents report local infrastructure problems in text or by voice and track their reports. Gemini translates descriptions, transcribes voice notes, and helps group nearby reports about the same need. A planning studio shows how those needs could fit an illustrative budget, with clear reasons for their priority. The prototype uses Google Gemini, Cloud Firestore, and Next.js. Its public planning examples are synthetic; live reports are kept in a private planner workspace.

## Before submitting

Open the GitHub link, video link, and prototype link in a signed-out browser. Check that the PDF is below 5 MB and opens correctly. Keep API keys, the planner passcode, service-account files, and private citizen reports out of the recording and slides.
